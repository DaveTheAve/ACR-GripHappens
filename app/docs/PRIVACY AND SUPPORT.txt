PRIVACY AND SUPPORT

Grip Happens has no account system and uploads nothing automatically.

Advanced -> Preview support report shows the complete sanitized JSON before you save it.
The sanitized report excludes usernames, local paths, Steam IDs, saves, raw logs, memory
snapshots and game files. Damage snapshots/driving recordings are separate diagnostics and
contain more game-state detail; share those only when you intentionally choose to.

Support page:
https://buymeacoffee.com/davethegeek

When reporting a problem, include Grip Happens 1.0.1, the exact error text, and the sanitized
support report when relevant. Unsupported ACR builds should be left untouched rather than
forced.
